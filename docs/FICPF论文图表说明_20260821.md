## 图 1：FICPF 方法总览

文件：

- `figures/ficpf_20260821/ficpf_method_overview.pdf`
- `figures/ficpf_20260821/ficpf_method_overview.svg`
- `figures/ficpf_20260821/ficpf_method_overview.png`

建议位置：Methods 末尾或方法章节开头，作为论文主方法图。

英文图注：

> **Figure X. Overview of Frozen Inductively Calibrated Pathomic Fusion (FICPF).** A matched VLSA-CONCH model provides a frozen pathology survival curve and its mean predicted survival time, while a compact molecular branch uses signature-linked RNA-seq and copy-number features, outer-training standardization, PCA64, and an L2-regularized Cox readout. Each modality-specific time score is mapped to a percentile by an empirical CDF fitted exclusively on the corresponding outer-training fold. The calibrated scores are combined using a fixed 0.80/0.20 pathology-to-omics weight, with explicit pathology fallback when molecular data are unavailable. A positive monotone Cox-Breslow projection fitted on outer-training patients yields complete survival curves without changing the FICPF ranking. Held-out labels are not used for fitting, calibration, fusion-weight selection, or curve reconstruction.

使用边界：图中的“frozen pathology branch”表示 FICPF 阶段不再联合训练 matched VLSA，而不是声称原 VLSA 从未训练。`65 Cox params` 只统计 PCA64 后 Cox 的 64 个权重和 1 个偏置，不包含 StandardScaler、PCA 和 ECDF 的无监督状态。完整多模态成本仍包含已有病理特征和 RNA/CNV 的获取。

## 图 2：五数据集统一外部对照

文件：

- `figures/ficpf_20260821/ficpf_main_comparison.pdf`
- `figures/ficpf_20260821/ficpf_main_comparison.svg`
- `figures/ficpf_20260821/ficpf_main_comparison.png`

建议位置：论文主结果图。

英文图注：

> **Figure X. Unified outer five-fold survival prediction comparison.** (A) Mean C-index of the published VLSA reference, the matched VLSA reproduction, MCAT-CONCH, CAT-TPL-CONCH, and FICPF across five TCGA cohorts and their macro average. All reproduced methods are evaluated using the same patient splits and the SurvivalEVAL continuous-time comparable-pair contract. (B) Paired patient-level bootstrap estimates for FICPF versus the matched VLSA baseline. Error bars denote 95% confidence intervals; filled markers indicate intervals excluding zero. FICPF achieves the highest mean C-index on all five cohorts and a macro-average gain of 0.0092 over matched VLSA.

使用边界：Panel B 只显示 FICPF 相对 matched VLSA 的配对区间。FICPF 相对 MCAT-CONCH 和 CAT-TPL-CONCH 的宏平均区间虽也排除零，但没有画在该 panel 中，应在正文或表格中报告。Published VLSA 只是论文参考值，不参与患者级配对 bootstrap。

英文图注：

> **Figure X. Component ablation and prespecified sensitivity analyses.** (A) Macro-average C-index for pathology-only prediction, omics-only prediction, uncalibrated fixed fusion, equally weighted ECDF fusion, and the locked FICPF configuration. (B) Sensitivity to the fixed omics fusion weight; the shaded region denotes the stable 0.10-0.30 interval and the diamond marks the locked weight of 0.20. 使用边界：Panel A 与 B 使用正式锁定 FICPF 结果，Panel C 使用统一本地依赖下的重拟合敏感性结果。PCA64 重拟合宏平均为 `0.705320`，正式主结果为 `0.705017`，两者不能在同一表中混写为完全相同的运行。

## 图 4：生存曲线质量

文件：

- `figures/ficpf_20260821/ficpf_survival_curve_quality.pdf`
- `figures/ficpf_20260821/ficpf_survival_curve_quality.svg`
- `figures/ficpf_20260821/ficpf_survival_curve_quality.png`

建议位置：生存曲线质量小节，作为排序指标之外的补充主证据。

英文图注：

> **Figure X. Multi-metric comparison of survival-curve quality.** Matched VLSA and FICPF are compared across five cohorts using (A) integrated Brier score, (B) Kaplan-Meier calibration error, (C) hinge mean absolute error, and (D) the number of outer folds passing D-calibration. Arrows indicate the preferred direction. FICPF improves probability error and population-level calibration but worsens absolute-time error.

使用边界：该图必须保留 MAE 退化，不能只展示 IBS 和校准改善。D-calibration 的通过折数是基于预设显著性阈值的描述性统计，不应解释为所有个体生存曲线均已准确校准。

## 图 5：测试时组学损坏鲁棒性

文件：

- `figures/ficpf_20260821/ficpf_omics_robustness.pdf`
- `figures/ficpf_20260821/ficpf_omics_robustness.svg`
- `figures/ficpf_20260821/ficpf_omics_robustness.png`

建议位置：正文鲁棒性小节或 Supplementary Figure。

英文图注：

> **Figure X. Robustness to test-time omics corruption.** C-index gains over pathology-only prediction under (A) random feature dropout with training-mean imputation, (B) Gaussian noise in the training-standardized feature space, and (C) patient-level omics missingness with pathology fallback. Fold-specific preprocessing, PCA64, Cox fitting, and ECDF calibration are learned once from outer-training patients and remain fixed for all corruptions. Random conditions are repeated 20 times without using survival labels. The black curve denotes the equally weighted macro average across cohorts. FICPF retains a positive macro gain under moderate corruption, whereas UCEC is sensitive to feature-level corruption because of its limited omics coverage.

使用边界：该图诚实保留 UCEC 在高特征缺失和强噪声下的负迁移。患者级缺失实验包含显式病理回退，因此 100% 缺失时增益按定义为零；不能将这一点表述为模型在没有组学输入时仍产生多模态增益。

## 图 6：固定阈值 OOF 风险分层

文件：

- `figures/ficpf_20260821/ficpf_oof_km_risk_stratification.pdf`
- `figures/ficpf_20260821/ficpf_oof_km_risk_stratification.svg`
- `figures/ficpf_20260821/ficpf_oof_km_risk_stratification.png`

建议位置：正文主结果末尾，用于把 C-index 改善转化为更直观的临床风险分层证据。

英文图注：

> **Figure X. Fixed-threshold out-of-fold risk stratification.** (A-E) Kaplan-Meier curves for high- and low-risk patients in the five TCGA cohorts. Each patient is scored by the outer-fold model that did not use that patient for fitting. High risk is defined prospectively as a calibrated fused percentile below 0.50; no survival outcome is used to optimize the cutoff. Censoring marks are shown on the curves. (F) Unadjusted and clinically adjusted Cox hazard ratios with 95% confidence intervals. Adjustment uses age, identifiable sex, and sufficiently represented OncoTree histologic subtype; adjusted estimates use complete cases.

使用边界：高风险阈值固定为融合分位数 `<0.50`，不是在测试结局上搜索最优 cut-off。临床校正包含年龄、可辨识性别和样本量充分的 OncoTree 组织学亚型，但没有分期和治疗变量；该图支持队列内 OOF 风险分离，不等同于完整临床独立性或外部验证。

## 输出规范

- PDF 和 SVG 用于论文排版与后续编辑，文字保持矢量或可编辑字体。
